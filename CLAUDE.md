# Rules for Claude on thekccf.org

Rena Koenig (KCCF) decides; she is not technical. Explain in plain words.

1. Facts: only from "KCCF Current Facts – MASTER" (Google Drive,
   00_KCCF_HQ_CLAUDE). Skip anything marked NEEDS INPUT or CONFIRM.
   Whenever a fact is new, changed or different anywhere (the website,
   a form, Monday, a document, or something Rena says in chat), remind
   Rena to update MASTER first, and give her the exact wording to add.
   Use the fact only after it is in MASTER.
2. Check before you recommend (rule 17): before saying something is wrong
   or needs changing, check it on the live site: every page it applies to,
   computer and phone, top of the page and after scrolling, and against
   MASTER. Say what you checked. Mark anything unchecked "not checked".
3. Time (rule 16): all dates and times are New York time. Convert from UTC
   before writing "today" or "tomorrow".
4. Every item gets a fixed code. Show at most 5 at a time, in a table,
   with your pick marked. Show before/after pictures with changes in red.
5. Safety rounds: Round 1 tsc, eslint, jest, build. Round 2 check every
   page. Round 3 live check after merge.
6. Rena clicks Merge. Claude prepares draft PRs and never merges.
7. Record decisions and changes in the KCCF Website Map.
8. Before updating any document, ask which one (rule 15).
